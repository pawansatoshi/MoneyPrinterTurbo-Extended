# Sats Terminal — PawanStudio Production Brief

## Non-negotiable source policy

For Sats Terminal videos, do NOT use user-supplied screenshots of Sats Terminal as product evidence when the same material can be retrieved from the official Sats Terminal website, official blog, official FAQ/docs, or official product pages.

The project source of truth is first-party Sats Terminal content. Discover and refresh official pages before every production. Prefer the official website, blog, FAQ/docs, product pages, and official announcements. Every product-specific visual must carry source URL and retrieval metadata.

## Asset hierarchy

1. Official Sats Terminal logo/brand asset.
2. Official Sats Terminal product/UI imagery from first-party pages.
3. Official Sats Terminal blog/article imagery.
4. Official Sats Terminal charts/data only when the source and date are recorded.
5. Licensed third-party factual/archival media.
6. AI-generated conceptual visuals only for abstract ideas and atmosphere.

AI must NEVER fabricate or replace an official Sats Terminal logo, dashboard, Loan Composer, rate, APY, balance, partner logo, product screenshot, announcement, or other product evidence.

## Creator identity

The creator's uploaded source photo is the identity master. Preserve the person's identity. Outfit/styling may be adapted per video's editorial context, but never replace the creator with a different person. Prefer real creator footage when available. Do not silently synthesize a different face or voice.

## Creative direction

Do not build a screenshot slideshow. Use editorial pacing: hook, question, reveal, product proof, explanatory graphics, visual metaphors, real UI demonstrations, purposeful camera movement, sound design, and a memorable payoff. Official UI should be animated through controlled crops, zooms, highlights, cursor/focus movement, and composited callouts rather than simply displayed as full-frame static images.

## Research facts for the current production

Sats Terminal's official pages currently describe it as a Bitcoin-native platform combining Trade, Borrow, and Earn. Borrow is described as a BTC-backed stablecoin lending aggregator that compares rates/terms across providers. Earn covers curated yield vaults for BTC and stablecoins. Official pages also emphasize non-custodial control and disclose liquidation/volatility risks.

Do not hard-code time-sensitive rates, liquidity, launch dates, or availability into evergreen creative. Fetch and cite current values at production time.

## Mandatory release gate

The render is NOT deliverable unless all of these pass:

- first-party asset provenance verified
- no user screenshot substituted for available official asset
- creator identity verified
- voice provenance verified
- every factual claim mapped to current evidence
- no fabricated product UI/data/logo
- frame/audio/subtitle QC passed
- no slideshow-only scene sequence
- narration/visual correspondence reviewed
- risk disclosure present where financial claims require it
- final director review passed

Any failure triggers targeted repair and a complete re-check before delivery. Missing analysis is BLOCKED, never PASS.
