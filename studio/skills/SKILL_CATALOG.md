## Quality skills

Every render should be eligible for automated rejection when it contains:

- black bars or unintended letterboxing
- unreadable or oversized captions
- text/UI collisions
- important UI hidden by overlays
- excessive camera motion
- repeated visuals
- silent/clipped audio
- narration/music imbalance
- missing CTA when required
- unsupported factual claims
- incorrect output dimensions/FPS/codec

## Design principle

Skills are composable. A project may combine, for example:

`cinematic + product_demo + tech_explainer + word_pop + macro_ui + documentary_pacing`

The Studio should never imitate a named creator's voice, face or copyrighted signature content. Creator references are translated into abstract production attributes such as pacing, shot density, typography restraint, camera energy and narrative structure.
